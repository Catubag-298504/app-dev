# Movies and Series I've Recently Watched

## Introduction
I don't really have one permanent favorite movie or series. My preferences tend to change depending on what I have watched recently. Sometimes, a particular movie or series sticks with me for a while, but that feeling eventually gets replaced when I discover and watch something new.

This README contains some of the movies and series I have watched recently, along with my personal thoughts and opinions about them.

---

## 1. Spider-Man: Brand New Day
![Sipder-Man: Brand New Day Poster](https://www.sonypictures.com/sites/default/files/styles/max_860x460/public/title-key-art/spidermanbrandnewday_onesheet_1400x2100_1.jpg?itok=hXKCyx3k)

**Type:** Movie

**Genre:** Superhero / Action / Adventure

### Why I Liked It
One of the reasons I particularly liked this movie was that it had been around four years since the previous movie, *Spider-Man: No Way Home*.

I found the conflict between Peter Parker's personal identity and his responsibilities as Spider-Man interesting. This concept reminded me of Sam Raimi's *Spider-Man 2*, where Peter goes through a difficult phase in which his powers begin to malfunction.

Another thing I enjoyed was the introduction of mutants, particularly **Jean Grey**. This made me more interested in how the story could connect to the wider Marvel Cinematic Universe, especially with *Avengers: Doomsday*.

### My Favorite Moment
> The moment that stood out to me the most was when Peter finally accepted both sides of his identity: Peter Parker and Spider-Man.
> This was particularly memorable because he underwent a significant physical and power transformation, which eventually led to him defeating **The Hand**.

### Overall Impression
Overall, I think the movie did a great job with its story and runtime. It didn't feel particularly rushed, and I was able to enjoy the progression of the story. However, I think some parts of the movie could have benefited from better pacing, although that is just my opinion.

**My Verdict:** A great Spider-Man movie with an interesting story, although there is still some room for improvement in its pacing.

## 2. Mobile Suit Gundam SEED / SEED Destiny

![Mobile Suit Gundam Seed Poster](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/gundam/images/c/c7/Gundam_SEED_Poster.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20190330035554)

![Mobile Suit Gundam Seed Destiny Poster](https://gundam-official.com/media/Destiny_kv_main001_comp_with_Logo_52865aaa4a/Destiny_kv_main001_comp_with_Logo_52865aaa4a.png)

**Type:** Anime Series

**Genre:** Mecha / Military Science Fiction

### Why I Watched It
I watched this series recently, mainly because I was playing a game based on it. I wanted to understand the background and context of the series before getting more involved with the game.

Although the game covered some of the story and lore, I wasn't particularly interested in reading large amounts of text displayed over still images. Because of that, I decided to watch the entire series instead.

For some background, **Gundam** is a long-running Japanese mecha and military science fiction franchise created by Yoshiyuki Tomino and Sunrise. It originally began with *Mobile Suit Gundam*, which I recommend you to watch and first aired on April 7, 1979.

*Mobile Suit Gundam SEED* first aired in 2002, followed by its continuation, *Mobile Suit Gundam SEED Destiny*, in 2004.

I had already watched a couple of other Gundam series before this one, so I decided to give SEED a try.

### My Thoughts on Gundam SEED
My overall impression of *Gundam SEED* is pretty good. I particularly enjoyed the story, characters, and the overall setting of the series.

However, one thing I didn't particularly like was the amount of **plot armor** given to the main protagonist. There are several situations where the protagonist seems almost indestructible, which makes certain encounters less convincing.

In fact, the amount of plot armor has become a popular subject for memes within the Gundam community.

### My Thoughts on Gundam SEED Destiny
Now, when it comes to *Gundam SEED Destiny*, I have a somewhat different opinion.

I personally think this continuation is rather mediocre compared to the original SEED. One of my main issues is that I don't particularly like the main protagonist, Shinn Asuka, as much as I liked the protagonist from the first series.

While I understand that the sequel wanted to introduce a different perspective and continue the story, I just didn't find it as enjoyable as its previous one.

### Overall Impression
Despite my criticisms, I still think the SEED series is worth watching, especially when considering both the original series and its continuation.

My preference, however, is definitely **Gundam SEED over Gundam SEED Destiny**.

**My Verdict:** A pretty enjoyable mecha anime series, although its sequel didn't quite live up to my expectations.
